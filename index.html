A little about me

Meet your nail tech

I fell in love with nails messing around with polish and designs among friends, and that hobby quickly turned into hands on training as an apprentice at a salon, I haven't looked back since. What I love most is the creative process, dreaming up a design and watching a client's face light up when they see the finished set. My favorite work leans dramatic: sculpted 3D details, charms, and long, bold shapes that make a statement.

I only work with vegan products in the studio, and every appointment is meant to feel like hanging out with a friend rather than a clinical treatment, grab a seat, relax, and let's design your next set together. For me, nails are just another way to express who you are.
